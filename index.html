Random Lists

A few starter lists (Names, Physical Traits, Contradictions, Fantasy Illustration Style) load automatically if reachable — remove any you don't want and they won't come back. Add your own too: plain-text, one entry per line, hosted at a raw URL — GitHub raw links (raw.githubusercontent.com) work reliably. Names lists power a blank @ in the log. Style lists feed the AI art-prompt line on character/location cards. Everything else appears as a quick-fill option under the 🧚 Fairy button. Lists are cached after loading, so a bad connection at the table won't break anything.
